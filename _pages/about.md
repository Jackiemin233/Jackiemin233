I am a Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence at the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)](https://www.hkust-gz.edu.cn/), advised by [Prof. Hao Wang](https://wanghao.tech). I first joined his group as a Research Assistant in April 2024 and stayed on for the Ph.D. Much of my work has asked how we can recover a person's 3D shape, appearance, and motion from limited observations: a single image, a few views, or signals from a head-mounted device. That has led me to work on 3D human reconstruction, avatar animation, and motion recovery. I am also interested in LLM agents, especially how language models can coordinate with specialized vision and action models.

Alongside my Ph.D., I am an Algorithm Engineer Intern at Horizon Robotics, working on video generation, world models, and vision-language-action research. I earned my B.S. in Network Engineering from [Zhejiang University of Technology](https://www.zjut.edu.cn/), where I studied with [Prof. Sixian Chan](https://homepage.zjut.edu.cn/csx/) and graduated from Jianxing Honors College. During my undergraduate years, I spent a term at the University of California, Irvine, worked on 3D reconstruction at [Zhejiang Lab](https://www.zhejianglab.org/lab/home), and explored Auto-ML and neural backdoor mitigation at Zhejiang University of Technology. I later worked remotely with [Prof. Zhengzhong Tu](https://vztu.github.io) at Texas A&M University on human video generation.
