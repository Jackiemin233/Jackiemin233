I joined [Prof. Hao Wang](https://wanghao.tech)'s group at the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)](https://www.hkust-gz.edu.cn/) as a research assistant in April 2024. My early projects focused on reconstructing a 3D person from a single image. The questions gradually moved from shape and texture to animation and motion, and I stayed for a Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence. I still spend most of my time on 3D human reconstruction and motion recovery, especially when the observations are sparse: one image, a few views, or signals from a head-mounted device.

Alongside that work, I am exploring LLM agents and how they can coordinate with specialized vision and action models. I am currently an Algorithm Engineer Intern at Horizon Robotics, where I work on video generation, world models, and vision-language-action research. I also worked remotely with [Prof. Zhengzhong Tu](https://vztu.github.io) at Texas A&M University on human video generation.

Before coming to HKUST (Guangzhou), I studied Network Engineering at [Zhejiang University of Technology](https://www.zjut.edu.cn/) with [Prof. Sixian Chan](https://homepage.zjut.edu.cn/csx/), and graduated from Jianxing Honors College. I spent a term at the University of California, Irvine, worked on 3D reconstruction at [Zhejiang Lab](https://www.zhejianglab.org/lab/home), and explored Auto-ML and neural backdoor mitigation during my undergraduate years.
