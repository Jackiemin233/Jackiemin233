I am a Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence at the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)](https://www.hkust-gz.edu.cn/), supervised by [Prof. Hao Wang](https://wanghao.tech). My research interests include 3D computer vision and large language model agents, with a focus on 3D reconstruction, human animation, and human pose estimation.

I am also an Algorithm Engineer Intern at Horizon Robotics, working on video generation, world models, and vision-language-action research. Previously, I was a Research Assistant at HKUST (Guangzhou), a remote intern at Texas A&M University with [Prof. Zhengzhong Tu](https://vztu.github.io), a part-time Research Assistant at Zhejiang University of Technology, and a Research Assistant Intern at Zhejiang Lab.

I earned a B.S. in Network Engineering from [Zhejiang University of Technology](https://www.zjut.edu.cn/) as an Honor Graduate of Jianxing Honors College. I also studied at the University of California, Irvine as a visiting student.
