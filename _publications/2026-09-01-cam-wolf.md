# Abstract

CaM-Wolf brings video, speech, and animated responses into a social deduction game agent. It combines a multimodal Perceiver with a Reasoner trained to distinguish causal evidence from misleading clues, then uses an avatar to communicate its decisions. The paper evaluates both gameplay and human-agent interaction.
