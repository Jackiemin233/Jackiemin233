# Abstract

This paper introduces SEHR, a single-view textured human reconstruction method that uses SMPL normal maps to guide a pretrained 3D reconstruction model. One component supplies body-shape guidance; another constrains the prediction of unseen body parts. The method reconstructs a clothed human in one forward pass without a preset diffusion model.
