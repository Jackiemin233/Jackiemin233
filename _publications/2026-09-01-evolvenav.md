# Abstract

EvolveNav studies zero-shot object-goal navigation with an agent that can improve during deployment. It extracts rules from past trajectories, weighs their usefulness, and uses memory-guided preflection to consider likely outcomes before moving. The public preprint reports improved success rates and fewer unnecessary steps against zero-shot baselines.
